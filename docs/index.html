Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

GuitarTuner respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use the application.

Microphone Access

GuitarTuner uses microphone access to detect the pitch of musical instruments and provide real-time tuning information.

Audio data is processed locally on your device. GuitarTuner does not record, store, upload, or share your audio recordings with third parties.

Data Collection

GuitarTuner does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The application does not require an account and does not collect user identifiers.

Tracking and Analytics

GuitarTuner does not use advertising services, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics services.

Third-Party Services

GuitarTuner does not share user data with third-party services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
